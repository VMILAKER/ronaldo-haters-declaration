International Treaty

This is a humorous, unofficial fan-made declaration and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or approved by Cristiano Ronaldo or any related organisation.

Declaration of
Cristiano Ronaldo’s Haters

International Treaty on Respectful Football Banter

Preamble

The Haters of Cristiano Ronaldo, inspired by the eternal debate over greatness, goals, trophies, and the mysterious power of “SIUUU”,

Recognising that Cristiano Ronaldo has scored an extraordinary number of goals, won major trophies, and provoked an equally extraordinary number of opinions;

Acknowledging that admiration, criticism, banter, and disbelief may coexist in the same football conversation;

Determined to establish fair rules for complaining about headers, free kicks, penalties, celebrations, fitness, and age;

Have agreed as follows:

Article 1 — Purpose

This Declaration establishes the rights, duties, and acceptable levels of dramatic sighing of all persons who identify as haters of Cristiano Ronaldo.

Article 2 — Definitions

Hater — a person who claims Ronaldo is overrated while secretly watching every match.

SIUUU — a globally recognised celebration.

Excuse — any explanation used after Ronaldo scores.

The Debate — the endless comparison with other footballers.

Article 3 — Right to Criticise

Every Hater has the right to criticise missed chances, defensive work, penalties, interviews, and hairstyle choices, provided the criticism is not abusive,discriminatory, or based on hatred toward any person.

Article 4 — Duty of Honesty

A Hater shall admit, at least once per calendar year, that some Ronaldo goals were, regrettably, quite good.

Article 5 — The Penalty Clause

No Hater may describe a penalty as “easy” after missing a penalty in a five-a-side match, school tournament, or video game.

Article 6 — The Age Clause

Comments about Ronaldo’s age must be updated annually. The phrase “he is finished” may only be used after independent verification that he has, in fact, stopped scoring.

Article 7 — Celebration Etiquette

Upon witnessing a Ronaldo goal, a Hater may remain silent, change the subject, or say “luck”. Shouting “SIUUU” is optional but emotionally risky.

Article 8 — Statistical Warfare

Haters may use statistics, provided they do not delete inconvenient statistics, invent trophies, or claim that a friendly match counts as a World Cup final.

Article 9 — Respectful Banter

Football rivalry is permitted. Personal abuse, harassment, discrimination, and threats are strictly prohibited under this Declaration and under basic human decency.

Article 10 — Entry into Force

This Declaration enters into force immediately upon the next controversial Ronaldo goal, whichever side of the debate the observer supports.

Concluding Provisions

Done in one copy, in the language of football, banter, and unnecessary replays. Each Hater receives one digital copy and one emotional recovery period.

Official Seal of the Haters’ Assembly
